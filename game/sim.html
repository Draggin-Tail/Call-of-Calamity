Call of Calamity — Balance Simulator

Reads your live /data/*.js files (1v1–6v6 squads, every battle map, height & rocks, 4 AP, CT casts, passive triggers, statuses & gear). Battles use the same rules and the same enemy AI as the game, with both sides AI-controlled, so it shows how fights play out in-game. Not covered: campaign objectives, enemy skill kits and uniques. Gear can be set to any of the seven tiers; uniques are not in the pool.
